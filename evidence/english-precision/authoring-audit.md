# Precision batch authoring audit

This is an authoring pass, not independent human editorial review. All 88 candidates remain pending.

- Two explicit four-context families extend every English skill. No new passage is counted solely because its choices are shuffled.
- Contrasting-quotation items require both sides of the stated interpretation; a pair of details supporting only one side is insufficient.
- Conditional evidence items distinguish the proposed interaction from a general performance improvement. The selected finding supports a prediction without proving a unique mechanism.
- Before/after tables distinguish endpoint and change rankings. Percentage-point changes are not relative percentage growth. Target/range tables keep inclusive bounds and all required cells explicit.
- Selection-effect inferences do not turn missing records into missing real events. Fixed-part inferences explicitly state the fixed whole, exhaustive parts and relevant exclusions.
- Cross-text definitions can yield different judgments without opposite measurements. Proposed tests are not completed findings; stated remaining design concerns are preserved.
- Synthesis keys provide the requested definition/example or the changed and unchanged dimensions. Dates, colors and storage labels do not replace the rhetorical goal.
- Residual limitations do not become consequences of the remedy. Alternatives do not imply that the first available route was refuted.
- Grammar cases identify shared subjects, required quoted complements, postverbal subjects and past reference events. The staff example does not falsely claim that collective nouns can never take plural agreement; its time reference is what rules out present perfect.
- Fictional quotations and dated scenarios are original educational contexts, not attributed real-world findings. Difficulty labels describe intended demands, not measured SAT performance.

Four initial quotation evidence spans omitted punctuation inside closing marks. Exact inner-word spans were substituted before the authoring commit, without changing an older question. An eager numerical-test helper attempted to read a third column from two-column tables; per-case lazy construction corrected the test without changing data. Failed authoring tests are retained separately from acceptance.

Numerical and structural checks are bounded. Human editors should challenge alternative grammatical parses, the naturalness and plausibility of every distractor, the strength of causal language, and whether the evidence establishes a uniquely best interpretation. No reviewer identity, publication or calibration is invented.
