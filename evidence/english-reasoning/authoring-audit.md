# Authoring audit, not independent editorial approval

All 44 candidates remain pending. The original passage/table and every option were checked during composition; automated checks are bounded and do not establish educational validity.

- Narrative reappraisal: keys describe the final change in action; they do not generalize one character's response into a rule about all notebooks, clocks, pauses or records.
- Exclusive explanations: the relevant comparisons challenge exclusivity. They do not establish a particular alternative mechanism as proven.
- Crossover data: independently recomputed two-condition orderings; kept score, angle, decibel and time units separate. Lower is favorable only where the claim explicitly requests lower measurements/times. No decibel/intensity ratio is inferred.
- Necessity controls: outcomes without the proposed condition defeat a universal necessity claim, not every possible contribution by that condition. The archival case states its own documentary acceptance criterion.
- Verb senses: contexts select consider, moderate, attract and limit. The distractor explanations address the local sense rather than label a word always incorrect.
- Concessions: specified sentences recognize limited usefulness. Their surrounding arguments still recommend supplementation or contextual interpretation.
- Cross-text scale: the pairs need not disagree about facts. The unit of analysis, rather than an invented policy preference, explains the keyed difference.
- Procedural synthesis: keys retain the order given in the notes and the requested purpose. Cover colors, labels and folder colors do not replace the actual method.
- Summary transitions: revised one awkward “Collectively” placement to “In sum” before committing. The same candidate ID was retained; all previously committed records remained unchanged.
- Complex series: the stated count of three outer elements and internal comma phrases motivate semicolon boundaries. A human editor should specifically challenge whether any unpunctuated alternative admits another defensible parse.
- Relative possession: whose links the following noun to its antecedent, including nonhuman antecedents. The keys do not claim that whose is restricted to people.

Final verification must include all keys and representative distractors through the real server, seed replay, old-record preservation, fresh-clone/repeat idempotency, and rendered phone/desktop layouts. These are prerequisites to review, not a substituted human decision.
