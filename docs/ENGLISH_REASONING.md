Authored 2026-10-28. This batch adds **44 original Reading and Writing candidates**, four per skill, in 11 finite families. The full bank now contains **861 English and 4,800 unchanged Math candidates: 5,661 total**. The method catalog contains **256 authored contexts in 64 families**. All 5,617 prior complete records are preserved.

## Structure before volume

| Skill | New structure | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central ideas and details | Narrative reappraisal | Infer a change of judgment from a character's actions without replacing it with an incidental detail. |
| Textual evidence | Exclusive explanations | Identify counterevidence to a sole-cause claim without asserting an untested alternative as proven. |
| Quantitative evidence | Crossover tables | Compare the same groups across conditions, preserving units and a reversal in ordering. |
| Inferences | Necessity and controls | Distinguish an outcome occurring without a condition from proof that the condition never matters. |
| Words in context | Local verb senses | Use surrounding actions and contrasts to select the relevant meaning of an academic verb. |
| Text structure and purpose | Concessions | Explain how a particular sentence grants value while maintaining a qualified criticism. |
| Cross-text connections | Units of analysis | Compare whole-system, local, component and vertical-level descriptions without inventing a contradiction. |
| Rhetorical synthesis | Procedures and purposes | Select relevant notes, preserve operation order and connect the procedure to the requested aim. |
| Transitions | Generalization | Connect specific observations to their shared broader significance. |
| Boundaries | Complex series | Separate outer list elements while respecting commas internal to each element. |
| Form, structure, and sense | Relative possession | Distinguish a possessive relative determiner from a contraction, object pronoun or nonpossessive relative. |

The public [College Board task guide](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice/student-question-bank/reading-writing) and [Assessment Framework version 4, Appendix B](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/assessment-framework-for-digital-sat-suite.pdf) were checked for task alignment. Passages, findings, tables, quotations, notes and options are original—not copied or paraphrased official questions. Fiction and hypothetical exercises are not reports of real research. Four options, one recorded key, explicit evidence and a task-aligned stem preserve the practice format without implying official endorsement or measured difficulty equivalence.

Each family contains four written contexts. Choice shuffling does not count as new authorship, and family clusters remain explicit. These items extend coverage; they do not establish that every possible SAT structure, reading demand or difficulty band is represented.

## Audit trail

The [44-item batch manifest](../content/manifests/english-reasoning.json), [batch review pack](../content/reviewed/english-reasoning.md) and [complete catalog](../content/blueprints/english-methods.json) retain IDs, seeds, methods, structures and pending states. The private studio's complete family facets expose the new families without a hard-coded first-page limit. Student payloads still exclude keys, methods, evidence and rationales before the appropriate graded feedback.

Independent numerical contracts recompute each table's ordering and compare the keyed statement with the correct group/condition cells. Decibel comparisons concern recorded levels only; they do not treat decibels as a linear intensity ratio. Structural grammar checks cover the bounded punctuation and relative-determiner patterns; they are not a general grammar parser.

Full-record digests protect every prior candidate and all Math records. Finite generation, seed replay, collision rejection, fresh-clone hydration and repeat idempotency remain mandatory. Earlier batch tests now select their own manifest IDs so later batches do not invalidate historical preservation checks. Generator version 3 retains its stable catalog timestamp convention; this batch's actual authoring date is recorded separately above and in its manifest name.

One precommit transition was revised from an awkward use of “Collectively” to the idiomatic summary “In sum.” Its draft record is retained; no previously committed item changed.
