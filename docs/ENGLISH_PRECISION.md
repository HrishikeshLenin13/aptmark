Authored 2026-10-28. **88 original candidates**, eight per English skill, extend the finite method catalog by **22 families**. The complete bank contains **949 English and 4,800 unchanged Math candidates: 5,749 total**. The method catalog contains 344 authored contexts in 86 families. ## Added structures

| Skill | Two new finite families | Intended reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central ideas and details | Competing aims; constrained detail sequences | Preserve both legitimate aims, or retrieve the specific step/condition requested. |
| Textual evidence | Contrasting quotations; conditional predictions | Supply both sides of an interpretation, or identify the pattern a proposed mechanism predicts. |
| Quantitative evidence | Baseline changes; explicit reference bounds | Distinguish an endpoint from a change, apply inclusive thresholds, and retain units. |
| Inferences | Selection effects; fixed whole and exhaustive parts | Separate records from the represented population, or infer a compensating change from stated constraints. |
| Words in context | Degree; relational verbs | Match strength/regularity and distinguish replacement, support, mediation and temporal order. |
| Text structure and purpose | Analogies; counterexamples | Explain what a local device contributes without treating illustration as proof or a limited example as universal. |
| Cross-text connections | Operational definitions; proposed tests | Compare criteria attached to the same term and distinguish proposed investigation from completed findings. |
| Rhetorical synthesis | Definitions; change with continuity | Select a definition and example for an unfamiliar audience, or retain both a revision and its explicit constants. |
| Transitions | Residual limitations; alternatives | Track an unresolved issue after an improvement, or a second available route without inventing a refutation. |
| Boundaries | Compound predicates; integrated quotations | Locate a shared subject or required complement before introducing punctuation. |
| Form, structure, and sense | Inverted agreement; past-perfect reference | Find the postverbal subject and place earlier completion relative to an explicitly past event. |

Public task descriptions in the [College Board Reading and Writing guide](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice/student-question-bank/reading-writing) and [Assessment Framework version 4, Appendix B](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/assessment-framework-for-digital-sat-suite.pdf) inform the format. No official question or passage is copied or reconstructed. Narratives, quotations, findings, tables and notes are original. Hypothetical data are not reports of real studies; the historical scenarios do not attribute invented actions to real people.

Four written cases per family are an explicit reuse cluster, not proof of semantic independence. Seeds determine stable choice ordering and do not manufacture additional passages. Intended difficulty labels are provisional, not measured SAT difficulty. The existing generator-version timestamp remains a stable catalog epoch; this batch's authoring date is recorded separately here and in the manifest name.

## Records and verification

The [batch manifest](../content/manifests/english-precision.json) lists all 88 IDs and actual answer-position counts. The [88-item review pack](../content/reviewed/english-precision.md) contains each passage, key, explanation, option-specific rationale, evidence and blank reviewer fields. The [complete catalog](../content/blueprints/english-methods.json) and private studio expose each method and structure while student serialization keeps keys and method metadata private before the appropriate graded feedback.

- Independent table contracts recompute baseline changes, including percentage points rather than relative percentage growth, and check every required cell against inclusive references. Group means are not evidence about every individual.
- Bounded grammar contracts check shared predicates, unbroken quoted complements, postverbal subjects and explicit past reference events. They are not a general-purpose grammar oracle.
- Full-record digests protect every earlier candidate and all Math records. Global contracts replay each registered seed and preserve pending/uncalibrated states.
- Browser checks grade all 88 keys plus one distractor per family against the real server, compare versions/hashes and inspect tables, quotes, notes, paired texts and grammar layouts on phone and desktop.
- Fresh-clone hydration and repeated generation must insert zero and preserve the bank bytes after the batch is complete.

Four draft evidence spans initially omitted punctuation inside closing quotation marks. Their exact inner-word spans were corrected before authoring commit or bank insertion. An initial numerical-test helper eagerly accessed nonexistent third columns for two-column tables; lazy per-case key construction fixed the test without changing the table data. Failed attempts remain separate from acceptance runs.
