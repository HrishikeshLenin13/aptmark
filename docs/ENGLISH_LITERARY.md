# Original literary reading

This cohort adds **24 original candidates** in six Reading and Writing families. Each family has four explicitly written contexts with stable disjoint seeds; choice shuffling does not create additional contexts. The batch supplements, rather than replaces, the bank’s existing coverage of all eleven English skills. Math remains unchanged.

| Skill | New close-reading mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Central Ideas and Details | Preserve two attitudes held in tension by an original poem |
| Textual Evidence | Select the diagnostic action from exact narrative quotations |
| Inferences | Separate observed conduct from an unestablished motive |
| Words in Context | Resolve context-specific sense and degree in fictional prose |
| Text Structure and Purpose | Explain how a final image reframes an earlier concern |
| Cross-Text Connections | Evaluate commentary against a poem’s sequence and qualifications |

The material includes four standalone original poems, four original poems paired with original commentary, and sixteen original prose scenes. No poem or fictional scene is an excerpt from a published work, and no real author is invented as its source. The private genre field and literary-interpretation review flag retain this provenance.

## Method and comparison
The [College Board public task guide](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice/student-question-bank/reading-writing) supplies skill/task conventions, not passage content. The [OnePrep purpose guide](https://www.oneprep.com/question-bank/reading-writing/text-structure-and-purpose) describes contextual function analysis. The cohort’s final-image questions therefore ask what an image contributes to its surrounding account rather than merely what objects it mentions. See [the dated comparison](WEBSITE_DIRECTION.md) for claim boundaries; neither source was used as a question template or passage corpus.

Every item includes four choices, a key, three option-specific distractor explanations, two reasoning steps, exact supporting spans, a method/heuristic and provisional difficulty. Heuristics are not SAT scoring formulas. The choice-length screen is descriptive: a long key is not automatically invalid, nor does matching lengths establish a good distractor. It is supplied for actual human inspection, not presented as semantic validation.

The manifest and pending pack at content/reviewed/english-literary.md record all 24 IDs and current keys. All 5,793 prior complete records and all Math records remain intact. The broader finite English catalog now has 412 contexts in 103 families; the whole bank has 1,017 English and 4,800 Math candidates.

## Open review
All 24 remain pending human editorial review, unpublished and uncalibrated. Automated quotation, replay, metadata, preservation, grading and rendering checks do not establish a uniquely defensible literary interpretation. Reviewers must inspect the strength and exclusivity of each reading, plausible distractors, fairness, tone and intended difficulty before any publication. No human approval or learning outcome is invented.
